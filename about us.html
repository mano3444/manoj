🧁 About Notes Bakery



Because good notes are like fresh cookies – best when shared!

📖 Our Story – How It All Started

“It was the night before exams. Notes were missing. Group chats were flooding. And that’s when the idea of Notes Bakery was born!”

In 2024, the idea of Notes Bakery was imagined by Rahul Kamboj, a student who deeply understood the struggle of finding good notes during exam time.

Later, this vision was turned into reality by Manoj Kumar, who designed and developed the entire platform — making sure the site is clean, fast, and student-friendly.

🎯 Our Mission

To make learning simpler, faster, and more accessible for every student — by providing well-organized PDF notes sorted by:

  • 🎓 University
  • 🧑‍🎓 Branch
  • 📅 Year
  • 📚 Subject
  • 📄 Chapter

📚 What We Offer

  • 🧁 Clean and easy to navigate interface
  • 📁 Downloadable PDF Notes
  • 📱 Mobile-friendly design
  • 🆓 100% Free – No login, no ads
  • 🔄 Regularly updated with new content
